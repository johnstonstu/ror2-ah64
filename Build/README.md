# AH-64 · The Gunship

An Apache helicopter survivor for **Risk of Rain 2**. Hover above the fight, track targets with your chin turret, and keep firing through rolls, backflips and banking turns.

[Full guide & skill videos](https://github.com/johnstonstu/ror2-ah64#the-kit) · [Changelog](https://github.com/johnstonstu/ror2-ah64/blob/main/CHANGELOG.md) · [Report a bug](https://github.com/johnstonstu/ror2-ah64/issues/new?template=bug_report.yml)

**Three guns. Three evasive moves. Three specials. Five paint schemes.** Your loadout changes the aircraft's visible equipment. Night Stalker unlocks through the Mastery achievement.

## Patch 1.3.3

Updated for **RoR2 1.5 / Hallowed Concepts**: restores character loading, rocket smoke and item displays, and fixes an audio shutdown crash. Includes updated dependency minimums. Keeps 1.3.2's reduced damage-hit knockback.

## Fly it

| Input | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| Move | Strafe above the terrain. |
| Hold Jump | Climb. |
| Descend · **C** keyboard / **B** controller | Drop toward your resting hover height. |
| Release climb & descend | Hold your current altitude. |
| Utility + movement | Choose your evasive direction while aiming and firing. |

The small crosshair gauge shows **airtime**. Return to resting hover height to refill it and collect ground items. Extra jumps extend your climb; kills briefly pause the drain. Optional Classic altitude controls restore release-Jump-to-descend flight.

## Build your loadout

| Slot | Choices |
| --- | --- |
| **Passive** | **Fire Control Radar** marks a strong nearby threat; gains damage, facing speed and close-range armor against it. |
| **Primary** | **M230**: balanced chain gun · **XM301**: sustained rotary fire · **M789**: slow, heavy explosive shells. |
| **Secondary** | **Hydra-70**: six-rocket ripple salvo. Your gun and rocket pods reload independently. |
| **Utility** | **Evasive Roll**: climbing roll · **Smoke Backflip**: rearward loop and cloak · **Banked Break**: sweeping 90° turn. |
| **Special** | **Longbow**: hold to paint locks, release to launch · **Hellfire**: hold to guide a live missile · **Bombing Run**: six bombs along your flight path. |

Keep firing your gun and Hydras while painting Longbow locks or shaping a bombing pass. With Hellfire, release to slow the lead missile and hold again to resume guiding it.

## Install & tune

Install through **r2modman** or **Thunderstore Mod Manager**, and let it install or update dependencies. This patch needs **SeekersPatcher 1.4.3+** and **MiscFixes 1.6.0+**.

For sliders and feedback tools, optionally install [Risk of Options](https://thunderstore.io/c/riskofrain2/p/Rune580/Risk_Of_Options/), then open **Settings → Mod Options → AH-64**. Use **Share settings → Copy & open GitHub** to share your setup; nothing is posted automatically.

**Multiplayer:** everyone needs the same mod version. Tuning is local; agree on matching settings. Remote and late-join missile-rack visuals still need dedicated testing.

**Languages:** English, 简体中文, Русский and Português (BR), following the game's language setting. Translations are machine-translated; corrections welcome. [Manual install & full instructions →](https://github.com/johnstonstu/ror2-ah64#install)
