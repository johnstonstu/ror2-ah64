# M230 private 1.3 playtest adjustment

Stuart's hands-on feedback on audited candidate `12a01e1`: the vanilla M230 still feels strong; reduce ammo count and slow the default fire rate slightly. This is a private release/1.3.0 iteration, not a public-release approval.

The actual 1.2.1/current defaults were 30 rounds and 0.10 seconds per round. Commit `0ea7b8f` previously changed cadence from 0.09 to 0.10 seconds and damage from 0.62 to 0.52, with HE from 0.34 to 0.26 and the close-range ramp. It left the 30-round drum unchanged.

This pass changes only M230 capacity 30 -> 24 (-20%) and interval 0.10 -> 0.11 seconds. Nominal rate at attack speed 1 becomes 9.09 rounds/second from 10 (-9.1%); this is a smaller change than the capacity cut. Per-round direct damage 0.52, HE 0.26, direct proc 0.50, zero splash proc, range/spread/recoil, 1.7-second reload and attack-speed/item scaling are unchanged. Other primaries, Hydra, missiles and movement are unchanged.

Ignoring fixed-tick scheduling, the full-range direct magazine payload falls 15.6 -> 12.48 damage coefficients. A nominal drum/reload cycle is 4.70 -> 4.34 seconds, so direct sustained output is 3.32 -> 2.88 coefficients/second (-13.4%). These are tuning arithmetic, not measured encounter DPS. More frequent reloads are intentional; do not compensate by buffing damage or proc coefficient.

Magazine size and M230 shot interval are hard-coded defaults consumed by the SkillDef/FireChaingun state, not config settings. Existing configs therefore cannot mask these two changes. Retain saved reload, damage, splash and other settings. No new config entries or balance migration are needed. The existing reload description derives its drum count from the tuning constant; the in-game primary tooltip already does likewise. English/translated public1.2.1 READMEs and historical changelog remain unchanged until the coordinated1.3 release documentation pass; translations belong to their separate owner.

The optional `AH64_AUTOPILOT_BALANCE_CHECKS=m230-v1` case operates only inside the existing two-gate diagnostic runner. It observes actual native M230 skill requests, one-stock costs, full capacity/empty blocking, distinct firing-state entries, fixed-tick cadence and whole-drum reload timing. It does not establish target damage, controller feel, alternate-primary gameplay or item scaling. The older audited candidate and artifacts remain historical evidence. New source/DLL/config/native evidence must be frozen and reviewed before retest; only the disposable AH64 1.3 Dev profile may be staged.
